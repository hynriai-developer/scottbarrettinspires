About Scott

Coaching built for working agents

Most agents don't need more motivation. They need a plan, a schedule, and someone holding them to it. Scott Barrett Inspires exists to give agents exactly that.

Coaching is direct and practical. You leave every session with specific actions for the week, not a notebook full of ideas. The focus is on the habits that actually produce closings: consistent prospecting, confident conversations, and clean follow-up.

Whether you're new to the business or established and plateaued, the goal is the same: a business that grows on purpose.

The coaching framework

The 4 Pillars of agent success

Every program is built on the same foundation: discipline, skills, systems, and passion. Strengthen all four and production follows.

Discipline

The separator between struggling agents and top producers. Daily actions tied to long-term goals, kept up even when motivation fades.

  • Time-blocked days
  • Daily minimum standards
  • Tracked numbers and accountability

Skills

Skills turn activity into results. Learn to prospect, communicate, build trust, and negotiate with confidence.

  • Daily role-play
  • Prospecting and listing presentations
  • Negotiation and objection handling

Systems

Systems separate hustling agents from business owners, making your business predictable, repeatable, and scalable.

  • Lead generation and follow-up
  • CRM and database nurture
  • Transaction management

Passion

The drive to persevere, build meaningful relationships, and stay committed through both challenges and opportunities.

  • Know your why
  • Celebrate progress
  • Serve clients for the long haul

FAQ

Common questions

Do you coach on AI?

Yes. Coaching is built on the four pillars of discipline, skills, systems, and passion, and it now includes advanced uses of agentic AI that help automate the repetitive parts of your systems.

Is coaching right for new agents?

Yes. New agents benefit most from building good habits early. Coaching is tailored to where you are.

I'm already producing. Can coaching still help?

Absolutely. Many agents plateau because their systems haven't kept up with their volume. Coaching helps you scale without burning out.

How much time will it take?

Plan on one session per week plus the daily actions in your plan, which are designed to fit around showings and appointments.

Do you guarantee results?

No coach can honestly guarantee income. What you get is a proven structure, accountability, and honest feedback. Results depend on the work you put in.
