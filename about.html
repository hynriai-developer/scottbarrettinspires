My story

From failing new agent to #1 producer to brokerage owner

Coach  |  Educator  |  Solutionist

I have been deeply invested in the real estate industry since 2004. Like many new agents, my journey didn't begin with immediate success. It began with failure. I felt lost, hopeless, and defeated, but one thing kept me moving forward: the determination to never give up.

That determination led me to seek out a real estate coach, and I chose the Mike Ferry System. The coaching, combined with consistent action and accountability, transformed my business and helped me become the #1 producing real estate agent in my local market.

In 2009, I went from top-producing agent to brokerage owner, earning enough through real estate sales to purchase the very brokerage where I had built my career, Florida Luxury Realty. From that day forward, my mission shifted from building my own business to helping others build theirs.

I have spent years supporting, educating, and coaching hundreds of real estate agents as they pursue their own definition of success, and it has been incredibly rewarding to watch so many become top producers, leaders, and respected professionals in their markets. My educational programs have been requested at every level of the industry, from local Realtor® associations and district events to presentations for Florida Realtors®.

Today, most of my coaching centers on two areas that determine long-term success: mindset and systems. These are the two greatest challenges most agents face, and mastering them builds the foundation for a thriving, sustainable business.
